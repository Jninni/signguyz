B · The white-label promise

We install under your brand. We never contact or sell to your customers.

Your customer gets your name, your card and your follow-up. Questions on site go back to you.

When a property owner asks us for a new sign, we refer them to a partner shop and install what they build.

Certificates of insurance and WSIB clearance sent on request, before the first job.

C · Sequence

How a trade job runs

  1. Drawings in

    Approved drawings, site address and permit status. Scope and price confirmed within 1 business day.

  2. Site survey

    Wall, power, access and lift position checked. Problems flagged to you before install day.

  3. Scheduled install

    Booked around your customer and the site. Our crane, our crew, our insurance.

  4. Photo report

    Before, during and after photos sent to you, unbranded and ready to forward.
